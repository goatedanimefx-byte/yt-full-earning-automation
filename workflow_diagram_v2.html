🚀 Auto-Improvements (Based on Past Data)

If low views:
• Change thumbnail style
• Try different topic keywords
• Shorter intro, hook faster
• Better title formulas
If low sales:
• Change product price
• Different affiliate product
• Better CTA placement
• More persuasive promotion
If high engagement:
• Make longer videos
• More controversial topics
• Series format works
• Keep that style
If mistakes happen:
• Log what went wrong
• Don't panic - fix and move
• Update system to avoid
• No hard rules - adapt
